## Cohort-level expectation flags (set early)
1. **Agents / building tools / scripting**: code-free course — scope, evaluate, govern; 8b no-code build is the closest to "building." Park deep agent/tool work.
2. **"Trust it every time"**: the course answer is the opposite — **verify, don't trust** (M3).
3. **Security depth**: CISO/ISSM room — the **M7 hijack demo** is the headline; give it room.
