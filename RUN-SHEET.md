**For:** the 9/22–9/23 delivery · **online** · solo instructor · one organization ·
ChatGPT Enterprise for all · **no breakout rooms**
**Built from:** the module decks, each exercise's own timing, and the 8b build lab.

> **Delivery is online and solo.** Run every exercise from **`ONLINE-DELIVERY.md`** — it is
> the authoritative version for these two days (individual-on-own-seat or whole-room; no
> teams, cards, sticky notes, or printouts). This run sheet gives the timeline; the online
> guide gives how each block actually runs on the call.

> **Timing, in short:** the course was over-full as originally designed (Day 2 by ~85 min),
> but **going online-solo removes team/breakout overhead and reclaims ~15–25 min/day**, and
> the whole-room capstone saves ~15–30 min more. With the cut plan below, **Day 2 now lands
> close to a standard day.** Confirm headcount and day length and you're safe.

## Timing reality — read this before you plan the cuts

| | Content demanded | Contact available | Over by |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Day 1** | ~396 min | 390 min | ~6 min + slippage |
| **Day 2** | ~475 min | 390 min | **~85 min** |

**Day 1** is tight but teachable — it goes over only if M2 and M4 (the two big exercises)
slip, which they will. Hold the debriefs to time and you land close.

**Day 2 was the real problem in-person** (~85 min over). **Online-solo fixes most of it:**
no breakout formation/teardown across the day (~15–25 min back) and a whole-room volunteer
capstone instead of six team pitches (~15–30 min back). That's roughly 30–55 min reclaimed
before you cut a single slide. Apply the light cuts below and Day 2 lands near 5:00 — but
still **confirm headcount and day length**, and still pre-decide the cuts so you're not
improvising at 3pm.

### The Day 2 cut plan (pick enough to reclaim ~85 min)

Ranked by how little they cost the learning:

1. **Capstone → right-size to your team count (−0 to −30 min).** The exercise file plans
   90 min for 6 teams; the outline says 60. With 4 teams, 5+5 min pitches = 60 min total.
   **Confirm your headcount and set the capstone length accordingly** — this alone may
   solve a third of the gap.
2. **Trim each Day-2 lecture ~20% (−25 min).** Execs don't need every slide. Pre-mark the
   skippable ones now (candidates: M5 org-structure detail, M6's four-reactions if the
   room gets it fast, M7 regulatory-landscape breadth, M8 vendor-evaluation list).
3. **Ex7 Incident Tabletop: 45→35 min working, hold debrief at 12 (−13 min).** It's a
   likely overrun; a hard timer per inject keeps it honest.
4. **Ex5 Maturity: fold the debrief into table talk (−8 min).**
5. **Protect 8b and its fail-then-fix beat.** It is the client's whole reason for the
   hands-on emphasis. Cut lecture and debrief minutes around it, not the build itself.

If you'd rather not cut: a **9:00–5:30 Day 2** buys 30 min, and starting the capstone
before the afternoon break (so it can't be squeezed) buys discipline. Cutting *and* a
slightly longer day is the safe combination.

## Pre-flight checklist — do this the day before (9/21)

**Tooling (the parts that fail live):**
- [ ] Confirm every participant will be logged into their **ChatGPT Enterprise** seat.
- [ ] **Confirm participants can create Custom GPTs** in the tenant (Explore GPTs →
      Create). If locked to admins, plan 8b's fallback (Projects, or single-chat upload).
- [ ] MTS **source packet** ready as an **uploadable file** — PDF *and* a plain-text/docx
      export (markdown doesn't always upload cleanly).
- [ ] Ex2 document pack A–D in the **shared class folder** (not printed).
- [ ] **Pre-build the grounded assistant** used in the M7 hijack demo, and **pre-run** the
      Ex2 prompts and the 8b build — screenshot each, as your outage insurance.
- [ ] Decide and note your **8b fallback** given what the tenant actually allows.

**Room & timing decisions (make these now, not live):**
- [ ] **Headcount / team count confirmed** → sets capstone length and whether Day 2 fits.
- [ ] Day length confirmed (9:00–5:00 or 5:30?) → drives how much you must cut.
- [ ] Day-2 lecture slides to skip **pre-marked** in the deck.
- [ ] Capstone length set (60 / 75 / 90) and a **visible timer** ready.

**Print (paper is now outage-insurance, not the plan):**
- [ ] Ex1 claim cards (6 sets), Ex3 packet + outputs (6, answer key instructor-only),
      Ex4 worksheets (6, A3), Ex5 worksheets (24), Ex6 role cards (6 sets), Ex7 injects
      (6 sets, separate sheets), Ex8 template (6, A3), capstone checklist (24) + scoring
      cards (24 × teams).
- [ ] A few paper Ex2 packs as backup only.

## The three live demos, and who drives

| Demo | Module | Who | Depends on |
|---|---|---|---|
| Prompt iteration (useless → usable) | M2 | Instructor | Any seat |
| Assistant gets hijacked | M7 | Instructor | A **pre-built** grounded assistant |

The M7 and M8/8b assistants are the *same idea* — build it once before Day 2, reuse it for
the hijack, then have the room rebuild their own in 8b. Make the continuity explicit; it's
one of the course's best moments.
