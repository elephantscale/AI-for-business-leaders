## S2 — MTS-PR-114 Rev B, clause 7.4 (verbatim extract)

> **7.4 — Vessels exhibiting readings below design minimum**
>
> Where examination identifies any measured value below the established design minimum,
> the vessel **shall not be returned to service** on the basis of a shortened
> re-examination interval, at full or reduced operating pressure.
>
> Return to service shall require, in sequence:
>
> (a) a fitness-for-service assessment carried out to MTS-PR-130;
> (b) written authorisation by the designated Technical Authority; and
> (c) entry of both (a) and (b) in the vessel record before service resumes.
>
> This clause departs from common industry practice, which permits return to service under
> a shortened interval in defined circumstances. **The departure is deliberate.** Rev A of
> this procedure permitted the shortened-interval route; that provision was withdrawn in
> 2025 following internal review.

## S5 — Policy statement 4.3 (verbatim, complete)

> **4.3 — Independence of examination and assessment**
>
> Meridian Technical Services maintains a separation between the personnel who carry out
> examination and those who determine fitness for service. This separation exists to
> ensure that the determination is not influenced by the commercial or schedule position
> of the examination activity. It applies to all engagements irrespective of client
> preference, and is not subject to waiver at engagement level. Where a client requests a
> combined arrangement, the request is declined and the reason recorded.

*This is the complete text of policy statement 4.3. There is no further text and no
associated procedure.*
