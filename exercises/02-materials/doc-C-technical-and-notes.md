**Extract from Examination Report HP-EX-0441, section 6.2 — Wall Thickness Findings**

Ultrasonic thickness measurement was performed at the lower circumferential weld of
vessels HP-A-118 and HP-A-141 using a 5 MHz dual-element probe on a 50 mm grid, with
supplementary readings at 25 mm spacing where the initial grid indicated local variation.
Couplant was applied throughout and surface preparation was to Sa 2½ where the existing
coating precluded direct contact.

Nominal wall thickness at this location is 14.2 mm with a design minimum of 11.8 mm
established by the original design calculation and unchanged by the revised standard.
Measured values on HP-A-118 ranged from 10.4 mm to 13.9 mm across 46 grid points, with 7
points falling below the design minimum and a minimum recorded value of 10.4 mm at grid
reference D4. HP-A-141 exhibited a comparable distribution, with 5 points below the design
minimum and a minimum recorded value of 10.9 mm at grid reference C4.

In both cases the sub-minimum readings cluster on the lower quadrant within 200 mm of the
weld toe, in a pattern consistent with preferential corrosion at a location of periodic
condensate retention rather than with a manufacturing deficiency. The morphology observed
on visual examination following coating removal supports this interpretation. The rate of
loss cannot be established from a single examination; the previous recorded examination of
either unit was in 2018 and the records for that examination consist of a scanned
handwritten summary without recorded measurement values.

Both units have been reported under the immediate notification provisions of the contract
and removed from service pending client determination. Continued service at reduced
operating pressure may be technically supportable but is outside the scope of this report
and would require a fitness-for-service assessment to the applicable standard.

Vessels HP-A-118 and HP-A-141 originate from manufacturing batch 1997-K, of which 21 units
are present in the examination population. Four further units from this batch have been
examined and cleared without adverse finding. The remaining 15 units have not yet been
examined.
